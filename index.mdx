Attesso is a server-to-server **authorization and evidence layer** for asynchronous agent actions. Your backend creates a bounded mandate. The end user approves it with a passkey. Attesso evaluates the concrete action your system proposes and signs the result into independently verifiable evidence.

Attesso does not execute an action or move money. You keep your own payment provider and bind each authorization to it.

## The complete flow

<Steps>
  <Step title="Create a mandate">
    Convert authenticated end-user intent into a deterministic policy with fixed action, attribute, payment, and time bounds.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Collect approval">
    Create a five-minute approval session and redirect the user to Attesso's hosted passkey ceremony.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Evaluate one action">
    Submit the exact action your system intends to perform. `DENY` and `INDETERMINATE` always fail closed.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Execute and close">
    Execute through your own systems before `execute_before`. Commit after acceptance, or cancel only after execution is impossible.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Verify the record">
    Retrieve and verify the signed evidence envelope independently of the Attesso dashboard.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Responsibility boundary

| Attesso | Your platform |
| --- | --- |
| Validates and stores the immutable mandate | Authenticates the end user |
| Hosts passkey approval | Converts intent into structured policy |
| Evaluates and reserves one execution | Performs the external action |
| Produces signed evidence | Reports external outcomes accurately |
